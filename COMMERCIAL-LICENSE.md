# Commercial License

Linux Doctor is free software released under the GNU General Public License v3
(see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The GPL is great for open-source use, but it
requires derivative works to stay open-source too.

**If you need to use Linux Doctor in a proprietary product** — for example,
embedding its checks in a closed-source application, or redistributing it
without GPL obligations — you can purchase a commercial license from the
author.

A commercial license grants you the right to use, modify, and redistribute
Linux Doctor in proprietary software without the copyleft requirements of
the GPL.

## How to buy

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/zShaD0w7x/linux-doctor-cli/issues)
or email the maintainer for pricing and terms.

## Why dual licensing?

- **Individuals and open-source projects** use Linux Doctor for free under
  the GPL-3.0.
- **Companies** that want to ship Linux Doctor inside their own closed-source
  products pay a commercial license — this is how MySQL, Qt, and many other
  projects fund their development while staying free for everyone else.
